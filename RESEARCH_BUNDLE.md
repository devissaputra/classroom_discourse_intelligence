# Research Bundle Evidence Contract

## Identity
**Area:** AI in Education  
**Study:** transcript-held-out teacher talk-move classification  
**Dataset:** TalkMoves, Suresh et al. (LREC 2022)

## Evidence required
A valid empirical run must record:
1. dataset source and license;
2. number of transcript files successfully parsed;
3. number of labeled teacher utterances;
4. human-label distribution;
5. transcript-level train/validation/test counts;
6. proof of no transcript overlap between splits;
7. majority baseline metrics;
8. TF-IDF logistic baseline metrics;
9. macro-F1 as primary metric;
10. per-class report and confusion matrix;
11. cleaning/skipped-file log.

## Non-claims
Classification performance does not establish teacher quality, instructional effectiveness, student learning benefit or validity outside English K–12 mathematics discourse.
